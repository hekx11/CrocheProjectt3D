Bear Head
Hook: 3 mm, DK (weight 3) acrylic, 9 mm safety eyes

HEAD
Rnd 1: 6 sc in MR (6)
Rnd 2: Inc in each st around (12)
Rnd 3: *Sc in next st, inc in next st; rep from * around (18)
Rnd 4: *Sc in next 2 sts, inc in next st; rep from * around (24)
Rnds 5-10: Sc around (24)
Insert safety eyes between Rnds 7 and 8, 5 sts apart.
Rnd 11: *Sc in next 2 sts, dec; rep from * around (18)
Stuff the head.
Rnd 12: (Sc, dec) x6 (12)
Rnd 13: Dec around (6)
Fasten off and close the hole.

EARS (make 2)
Rnd 1: 6 sc in MR (6)
Rnd 2: Inc around (12)
Rnds 3-4: Sc around (12)
Fasten off, leaving a long tail for sewing. Do not stuff. Flatten the ears and sew them to the top of the head between Rnds 3 and 6, about 8 sts apart.

SNOUT
Rnd 1: 6 sc in MR (6)
Rnd 2: Inc around (12)
Rnd 3: Sc in BLO around (12)
Fasten off, leaving a long tail. Stuff lightly and sew to the head below the eyes, centered between Rnds 8 and 11.
