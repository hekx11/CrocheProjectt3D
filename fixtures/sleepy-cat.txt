Sleepy cat
Hook: 3.5 mm, worsted (weight 4) acrylic, 6 mm safety eyes

Body (grey)
Ch 7.
Rnd 1: Starting in 2nd ch from hook, sc in next 5 ch, 3 sc in last ch, working on the other side of the chain, sc in next 4 ch, 2 sc in last ch. (14)
Rnd 2: inc, 4 sc, inc x3, 4 sc, inc x2 (20)
Rnd 3: (sc, inc), 4 sc, (sc, inc) x3, 4 sc, (sc, inc) x2 (26)
Rnds 4-10: sc around (26)
Rnd 11: (sc, dec) x8, 2 sc (18)
Stuff the body.
Rnd 12: dec x9 (9)
Fasten off and close.

Ears (make 2, grey)
Rnd 1: 4 sc in MR (4)
Rnd 2: (sc, inc) x2 (6)
Rnd 3: (2 sc, inc) x2 (8)
Fasten off. Do not stuff. Sew the ears to the top of the body between Rnds 3 and 4.

Tail (grey)
Rnd 1: 5 sc in MR (5)
Rnds 2-12: sc around (5)
Fasten off. Sew to the back of the body.
