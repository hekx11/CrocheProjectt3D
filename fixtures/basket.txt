Small storage basket
Hook: 6 mm, two strands of worsted cotton held together

Base
Rnd 1: Ch 3 (does not count as a st), 12 dc in 3rd ch from hook, sl st to first dc to join. (12)
Rnd 2: Ch 2, 2 dc in each st around, sl st to join. (24)
Rnd 3: Ch 2, *dc in next st, 2 dc in next st; repeat from * around, sl st to join. (36)
Rnd 4: Ch 2, *dc in next 2 sts, 2 dc in next st; repeat from * around, sl st to join. (48)

Sides
Rnd 5: Ch 1, sc in the back loop of each st around, sl st to join. (48)
Rnds 6-15: Ch 1, sc in each st around, sl st to join. (48)
Fasten off and weave in ends.
