Little bear
Hook: 3 mm, DK (weight 3) acrylic, 8 mm safety eyes

BODY
Rnd 1: 6 sc in MR (6)
Rnd 2: Inc in each st around (12)
Rnd 3: (Sc, inc) x6 (18)
Rnd 4: (2 sc, inc) x6 (24)
Rnds 5-12: Sc around (24)
Insert safety eyes between Rnds 6 and 7, 5 sts apart.
Rnd 13: (2 sc, dec) x6 (18)
Rnds 14-16: Sc around (18)
Rnd 17: (Sc, dec) x6 (12)
Stuff the body firmly.
Rnd 18: Dec around (6)
Fasten off and close the hole.

ARMS (make 2)
Rnd 1: 6 sc in MR (6)
Rnds 2-7: Sc around (6)
Fasten off, leaving a long tail for sewing. Stuff lightly.
Sew the arms to the sides of the body between Rnds 9 and 11.

EARS (make 2)
Rnd 1: 6 sc in MR (6)
Rnd 2: Inc in each st around (12)
Fasten off, leaving a long tail for sewing. Do not stuff.
Flatten the ears and sew them to the top of the body between Rnds 2 and 4, about 8 sts apart.
